## Requirements

- Register users and meeting rooms.
- Schedule meetings with a title, time slot, organizer, and invitees.
- Check attendee availability and prevent overlapping meetings.
- Automatically assign an available room with sufficient capacity.
- Support room selection strategies: **Smallest Fit** and **Largest Fit**.
- Maintain schedules for users and meeting rooms.
- Cancel meetings and update all associated schedules.
- Retrieve scheduled meetings and view user/room calendars.
- Prevent duplicate attendees and include the organizer in the meeting.
- Handle booking failures when attendees or rooms are unavailable.
